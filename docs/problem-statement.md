# Problem Statement

## Safe and Efficient Operation of Mine Vehicles in Fog and Low-Visibility Conditions in Open Cast Iron Ore Mines

### Objective

Develop a safety-oriented system for open-cast iron-ore mine vehicles that improves situational awareness during fog and other low-visibility conditions.

### Challenge

Reduced visibility can make it difficult for operators to detect vehicles, people, obstacles and changes in the haul-road environment early enough to react safely.

### Proposed Direction

The system combines sensing, computer vision and real-time risk analysis to provide timely warnings and support safer vehicle operation.

### Scope

- Detect relevant objects and hazards in the forward field of view.
- Estimate visibility/environmental conditions.
- Analyse distance and motion information where available.
- Generate graded warnings based on detected risk.
- Provide real-time monitoring of vehicle/system status.
- Keep warning decisions traceable to measurable inputs.

> Hardware and AI model choices can be adapted to the final prototype and field constraints.
